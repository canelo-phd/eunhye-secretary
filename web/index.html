ABOUT ANGEL CANELO

Dr. Angel Canelo

Staff Engineer at Samsung Electronics, developing deep learning-based solutions for image enhancement and restoration.

Angel earned a Ph.D. in Electronics Engineering from Hanyang University in 2024. His doctoral research explored insect brain-inspired algorithms for vision-based navigation, bridging computational neuroscience, computer vision, and autonomous systems.

His academic background also includes a Master’s degree in Industrial Engineering and a Bachelor’s degree in Electronics and Automation Engineering from the University of Extremadura. His work spans deep learning, computer vision, reinforcement learning, robotics, control theory, and embedded systems.
